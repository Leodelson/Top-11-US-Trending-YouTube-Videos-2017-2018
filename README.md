## Problem Statement:
YouTube’s trending algorithm highlights videos that gain rapid traction, but understanding *why* certain videos trend requires deeper analysis of viewer engagement patterns such as likes, comments, and views.  
Between 2017 and 2018, the U.S. YouTube space experienced major shifts driven by viral content, cultural moments, and music releases.  
This project aims to uncover what factors contributed to the top trending videos during this period and provide insights into channel performance, audience behavior, and engagement dynamics.

# Insights into U.S. YouTube Trends:

### Rising & Falling Trends:
- Peak popularity in **early–mid 2018**  
- Viewer preference leaned heavily toward **music videos** and **viral platform events** like YouTube Rewind

### Regional Dynamics:
- U.S. viewers consistently prefer:
  - High-production music videos  
  - Socially impactful or controversial content  

### Impact of Viral Trends:
Videos backed by strong social media discussion (Twitter, Instagram) performed particularly well.
